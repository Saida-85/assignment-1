# 🚀 DevConf 2026 Landing Page

A modern, responsive, and feature-rich landing page for **DevConf 2026**—a premier technology and developer conference bringing together over 4,000 engineers, founders, and tech builders. Designed with clean semantic HTML5 and styled with custom CSS, external typography, and vector icon kits.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Hero & Navigation Bar:** Includes custom branding, navigation links (`Speakers`, `Schedule`, `Tracks`, `Venue`, `Blog`), CTA registration buttons, and a dark overlay banner.
* **Featured Speakers Section:** Grid/card layout highlighting industry keynote speakers across key technical tracks (AI/ML, Cloud & DevOps, Frontend, and Security).
* **Tiered Pricing Table:** Includes three registration options (**Standard**, **Pro**, and **Team**) with detailed perk lists and early-bird callout notices.
* **Interactive FAQ Accordion/Cards:** Section covering venue details, laptop requirements, recording availability, and attendee skill levels.
* **Footer & Social Links:** Custom branding footer integrated with social media icons and legal policy navigation (`Privacy Policy`, `Terms of Service`).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integrations

* **HTML5:** Modern semantic markup (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
* **CSS3:** Custom stylesheet (`style.css`) for layout structure, typography, and theme styling.
* **Google Fonts:** Integrated **Inter** and **Open Sans** typography.
* **Font Awesome Kit:** Integrated vector icons for social media links.
